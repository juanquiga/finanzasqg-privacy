Privacy Policy – FinanzasQG

Last updated: 2026

1. Introduction

FinanzasQG is a personal finance management application designed to help users track, analyze, and optimize their financial activity. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal and financial data is handled securely and transparently.

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your information is FinanzasQG. For any privacy-related inquiries, contact: soporte@finanzasqg.com

3. Information We Collect

  • Financial data (income, expenses, transfers, balances)
  • Custom categories, subcategories, and configurations
  • Account information (account names, internal balances)
  • User authentication data (via Firebase Authentication)
  • Technical data (device type, OS version, app usage diagnostics)

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core app functionality
  • Generate financial analytics, reports, and projections
  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Enhance user experience

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your data based on:

  • User consent
  • Legitimate interest (app functionality and improvement)
  • Performance of a contract (providing app services)

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase services provided by Google (Google Cloud Platform). We apply industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and secure database rules.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service. Users may delete their data at any time within the application.

8. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties.

9. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Realtime Database / Firestore
  • Google Cloud infrastructure

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country, including the United States, through Google Cloud services.

11. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

12. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

13. Security Disclaimer

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no system is completely secure. Users are responsible for safeguarding their account credentials.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
